Rheumatic heart disease kills 373,000 people a year, mostly kids, and the cure is a penicillin shot that costs cents.

The strange part: coverage and stockout data exist mainly in low-burden countries. The four worst-hit (India, China, Pakistan, Indonesia) have no register and no documented stockout. The map is blank where it matters most.

Closing the gap in 5 high-burden countries: ~234,000 deaths avertable a year for an $18M–$168M drug bill. The whole world's supply rests on 3 plants in China. Fund that neck and the missing registers.
