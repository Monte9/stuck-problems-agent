# Methane super-emitters: the climate fix that pays for itself and still doesn't happen

> Intake brief from the scout routine, 2026-06-11. Domain: energy and climate.
> Bottleneck type: misaligned incentives plus attribution grind nobody is staffed to do — against a 2025 backdrop of enforcement going backwards.

**(a) The problem.** Methane causes roughly 0.5°C of current warming — about a third of the total — and, as the key precursor of ground-level ozone, it kills now, not in 2100: the UNEP/CCAC [Global Methane Assessment](https://www.ccacoalition.org/content/global-methane-assessment) finds a 45% cut would prevent **260,000 premature deaths, 775,000 asthma hospital visits, 73 billion hours of lost labour, and 25 million tonnes of crop losses every year**. The fossil-fuel sector emitted ~124 Mt in 2025 — [plateaued at record highs](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-methane-tracker-2026/key-findings) despite 150+ countries signing the Global Methane Pledge in 2021. A large share comes from a small number of "super-emitter" leaks, blowouts, and deliberate venting.

**(b) The half-known solution.** This is the rare climate problem where the fix is *profitable*. The IEA's [Global Methane Tracker 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-methane-tracker-2026/key-findings) finds ~85 Mt (70%) abatable with existing technology — dry seals, vapour recovery, leak detection and repair — and **more than 35 Mt at no net cost**, because the captured gas is worth more than the repair. Over [15 Mt of fixes carry internal rates of return above 25%](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-methane-tracker-2025/key-findings). Detection works too: satellites (Sentinel-5P, GHGSat, Carbon Mapper) spot individual plumes, and UNEP's [Methane Alert and Response System](https://www.unep.org/topics/energy/methane/methane-alert-and-response-system-mars) emails operators the coordinates.

**(c) Why it's stuck.** Operators ignore the alerts: only [1% of MARS notifications got any response in 2024, ~12% in 2025](https://www.unep.org/resources/eye-methane-2025-measurement-momentum) — and only about a third of those responses involved actual mitigation. Leaked gas is unmetered and unpenalized, so "no net cost" to society is still a cost line to a state oil company with no offtake pipeline. 2025 made it worse: [MethaneSAT died after one year in orbit](https://www.science.org/content/article/methane-tracker-lost-space) (June 20, 2025), Congress [revoked the US waste-emissions-charge rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/19/2025-08688/congressional-review-act-revocation-of-waste-emissions-charge-for-petroleum-and-natural-gas-systems) (CBO: $7.2B foregone), and industry is [lobbying to "simplify" the EU Methane Regulation's importer requirements](https://www.eurogas.org/resource/joint-industry-recommendations-for-simplification-of-the-importer-requirements-of-the-eu-methane-regulation/) before they bind in January 2027. The one lever with teeth — the EU buys the gas — [survived the 2026 omnibus](https://sustainableatlas.org/insights/2026/top-10-eu-sustainability-regulations-2026) but its MRV-equivalency machinery is wobbling for lack of exactly the synthesis work this brief proposes.

**(d) The AI-agent wedge.** The missing artifact is attribution and ranking, all desk work over open data: (1) cross-reference public plume detections (IMEO/MARS data platform, [Carbon Mapper's open portal](https://carbonmapper.org), Sentinel-5P) against facility ownership to produce a **named-operator super-emitter league table**, including who never responds to alerts; (2) build the **EU-importer dossier** — which exporting countries and companies supply EU gas, at what measured methane intensity — the document DG ENER needs to implement the import standard rather than dilute it; (3) reconcile the IEA's open [Methane Abatement Model](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-methane-tracker-2025) with satellite observations into a country-ranked list of no-net-cost fixes. The agent cannot fix a compressor seal, but it can end the anonymity that lets leaks persist.

**(e) Closest to cracking it.** UNEP IMEO (Manfredi Caltagirone) on alerts; Carbon Mapper (Riley Duren) and GHGSat on detection; EDF (Steven Hamburg) regrouping post-MethaneSAT with airborne sensors and GOSAT-GW; Clean Air Task Force and IEA on abatement economics; the European Commission holding the only binding import lever; Global Methane Hub funding the field. The detection layer is built. Nobody is staffed to turn plumes into names, and names into enforcement.
