# Dark fleets: the deadliest industry on Earth is fully visible from orbit and still anonymous

> Intake brief from the scout routine, 2026-06-12. Domain: frontier expansion — oceans.
> Bottleneck type: attribution grind nobody is staffed to do, compounded by misaligned incentives (flag states, anonymous owners) — against a 2025 backdrop of US enforcement going backwards while the EU's lever sharpens.

**(a) The problem.** Fishing kills more workers than any other industry: a 2022 FISH Safety Foundation study commissioned by Pew found [over 100,000 fishing-related deaths a year — more than 300 a day — over four times the old FAO/ILO figure](https://www.pew.org/en/about/news-room/press-releases-and-statements/2022/11/03/more-than-100000-people-die-annually-across-global-fishing-sector-new-research-shows), with illegal, unreported and unregulated (IUU) fishing a significant driver. At least [128,000 fishers are trapped in forced labor at sea](https://www.ilo.org/topics/forced-labour-modern-slavery-and-trafficking-persons/sectors-and-topics/forced-labour-and-human-trafficking-fisheries) (ILO; a known undercount), and a Global Fishing Watch model flagged [up to 4,000 vessels — including >75% of squid jiggers — with movement patterns matching known forced-labor cases](https://globalfishingwatch.org/blog/new-study-unveils-risk-of-forced-labor-in-fisheries). IUU fishing itself is worth [up to $23.5 billion a year](https://atfcp.com/2022/11/16/inadequate-due-diligence-and-beneficial-ownership-transparency-facilitating-us23-billion-illegal-fishing-industry/), draining [up to $11.5 billion from Africa alone](https://financialtransparency.org/half-illegal-fishing-vessels-operate-africa-majority-chinese-european-new-report/) — income taken from some of the poorest coastal communities on Earth.

**(b) The half-known solution.** Detection is solved. Global Fishing Watch's 2024 *Nature* study used Sentinel-1 SAR plus AI to map every large vessel at sea, revealing that [~75% of industrial fishing vessels are absent from public tracking](https://www.esa.int/Applications/Observing_the_Earth/Copernicus/Sentinel-1/Sentinel-1_and_AI_reveal_75_of_fishing_vessels_not_tracked) — and publishing the [detections as open data](https://globalfishingwatch.org/platform-update/2024-may-data-download-portal-new-dataset-released-featuring-vessel-detections-from-sentinel-1-sar/). Enforcement levers exist and work: the EU's carding system (28 yellow cards, 6 red since 2012) [measurably reformed Thailand, Belize and others](https://europe.oceana.org/reports/driving-improvements-fisheries-governance-globally-impact-eu-iuu-carding-scheme/), and two new instruments just went live: the [WTO Fisheries Subsidies Agreement (in force 15 September 2025)](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news25_e/fish_15sep25_e.htm) bans subsidies to operators engaged in IUU fishing, and the EU's [CATCH digital certification system (mandatory 10 January 2026)](https://www.iuuwatch.eu/2026/01/advancing-the-eus-fight-against-illegal-fishing-the-launch-of-the-it-catch-system/) digitizes 250,000 catch certificates a year with real-time risk alerts.

**(c) Why it's stuck.** Both new levers only bite against *named operators* — and naming is the unstaffed grind. The Financial Transparency Coalition found [shareholder data exists for only 16% of vessels implicated in IUU fishing; almost no country requires beneficial ownership at registration or licensing](https://financialtransparency.org/fishy-networks-challenges-uncovering-beneficial-owners-behind-illegal-fishing-global-south-countries/). Just ten companies own a quarter of identified IUU vessels — eight Chinese, one Spanish that collected EU subsidies. Meanwhile the US went backwards: [Executive Order 14276 (April 2025)](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IF13017) ordered the Seafood Import Monitoring Program "revised or rescinded," drawing criticism that it [unwinds forced-labor screening](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/08/27/2025-16377/recommendations-for-restoring-american-seafood-competitiveness). CATCH's risk alerts are only as good as the risk data fed in, and [member-state follow-up remains the weakest link](https://www.iuuwatch.eu/2026/01/advancing-the-eus-fight-against-illegal-fishing-the-launch-of-the-it-catch-system/).

**(d) The AI-agent wedge.** Pure desk attribution over open data, the methane playbook at sea: (1) cross-reference GFW's open SAR/AIS detections, RFMO IUU vessel lists, and port detentions against corporate registries to build a **named beneficial-owner league table** of dark-fleet and forced-labor-risk vessels; (2) a **DG MARE carding dossier** ranking the flag states exporting to the EU by dark-activity and forced-labor-risk profile — the evidence base for the next yellow-card round and for CATCH risk-flagging; (3) a **WTO Article 3 docket** tracing subsidy programs to operators on IUU lists, since the new agreement's prohibition is inert until someone connects subsidy to operator. The agent cannot board a vessel; it can end the anonymity that the entire business model depends on.

**(e) Closest to cracking it.** Global Fishing Watch (Tony Long) and its Joint Analytical Cell with Trygg Mat Tracking and the IMCS Network on detection-to-analysis; the Financial Transparency Coalition on ownership; the Environmental Justice Foundation (Steve Trent) and Outlaw Ocean Project (Ian Urbina) on documentation; the European Commission's DG MARE holding the only binding market lever; Pew and Oceana on policy. The satellites watch everything. Nobody is staffed to turn detections into owners, and owners into cards.
