## Criterion 2 — The casualty-risk formula is written explicitly, every variable defined, with at least one peer-reviewed or agency citation

MET.

The formula is written out, not merely named, at three levels:
- B0 casualty area: "Aᶜ = Σᵢ ( √Aₕ + √Aᵢ )²" with Aᶜ, Aᵢ, Aₕ (0.36 m²) all defined and the 15 J lethality convention stated.
- B1 expected casualties: "Eₐ = Σ_k P_impact(k) · ρ_pop(k) · Aᶜ" with every term (Eₐ, P_impact, ρ_pop, Aᶜ) defined and the 1×10⁻⁴ threshold stated.
- B2 latitude-resolved form: "CE = Σ_φ w(φ) · ρ_pop(φ)", "Eₐ ≈ CE · Aᶜ" with CE, w(φ), ρ_pop(φ) defined.

Each block carries agency/peer-reviewed citations: NASA-STD-8719.14 (agency standard) and FAA AC 431.35-2A (agency) for B0/B1, and Byers, Wright & Boley, Nature Astronomy 6:1093–1097 (2022), DOI 10.1038/s41550-022-01718-8 (peer-reviewed) plus Pardini & Anselmo, J. Space Safety Eng. 11(2) (2024) for the threshold and B2. Spot-checks confirmed: the Byers paper title/authors are correct; the NASA ORSAT page confirms the casualty-area concept and the 1-in-10,000 threshold computed from casualty area, inclination, and reentry year — matching the artifact's use.

## Criterion 3 — Controlled-vs-uncontrolled classification lists concrete signals and states how ambiguous/unknown cases are handled

MET.

Section C lists concrete, catalog-derivable signals: CONTROLLED signals 1–3 (operator-declared deorbit, SPOUA/Point Nemo targeted-ocean footprint, propulsive restartable stage with retained fuel); UNCONTROLLED signals 4–6 (launch-to-reentry span ≥ 7-day dwell threshold per Byers et al., no disposal capability, random non-targeted decay). The decisive test is explicit: "< 7 days → candidate controlled ... ≥ 7 days → uncontrolled."

Ambiguous/unknown handling is explicit and multi-branch: still-in-orbit bodies are labelled separately and treated as future-uncontrolled only if no disposal capability and perigee ≤ 600 km; and "If control status genuinely cannot be resolved from any signal above, the body is defaulted to UNCONTROLLED and explicitly flagged control_basis = default-uncontrolled," with the count required to be reported in M2.

## Criterion 4 — Scope pinned to explicit date window and unit of analysis; numbered limitations section with ≥3 distinct caveats

MET.

Date window: "1 January 2013 – 31 December 2024," with named reconciliation sub-windows (2010–2022, 2019–2023). Unit of analysis: "one abandoned rocket body / upper stage" with an explicit Included list (GCAT R/R/B; one row per catalogued rocket-body object) and an Excluded list (payloads, suborbital stages, mission/fragmentation debris; controlled-deorbit stages kept as labelled rows).

The numbered Limitations & counter-evidence section has 6 distinct, substantive caveats: (1) ≥7-day proxy misclassification, (2) casualty-area Aᶜ as dominant uncertainty, (3) latitude-only ground-track reduction, (4) incomplete/source-dependent mass and control coverage correlated with high-risk families, (5) access-gated sources, (6) counter-evidence that no confirmed casualty has occurred. Exceeds the ≥3 requirement and engages adversarially rather than with boilerplate.
