## Criterion 2 — The casualty-risk formula is written explicitly with each variable defined and at least one peer-reviewed or agency citation.
MET. Section B writes three explicit equations, not just names them:
- B0 casualty area: Ac = Sum_i (sqrt(Ah) + sqrt(Ai))^2, with Ac, Ai, Ah all defined (Ah = 0.36 m^2 human circle), lethal-fragment >=15 J convention stated.
- B1 expected casualties: Ea = Sum_k P_impact(k) * rho_pop(k) * Ac, every variable defined, with the 1e-4 compliance threshold.
- B2 latitude-resolved: CE = Sum_phi w(phi) * rho_pop(phi), Ea ~ CE * Ac, w(phi) defined via inclination.
- Citations are agency + peer-reviewed: NASA-STD-8719.14 (agency standard), FAA AC 431.35-2A (agency), and Byers/Wright/Boley Nature Astronomy 2022 (peer-reviewed, DOI 10.1038/s41550-022-01718-8) and Pardini & Anselmo J. Space Safety Eng. 2024 (peer-reviewed).
